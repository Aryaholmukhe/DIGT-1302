Topic 01

General Questions

What is GradeGuard?

GradeGuard is a student-focused academic planning tool. It helps university students track course grades, calculate weighted progress, test target-grade scenarios, and estimate their overall GPA.

Who is this website for?

The main audience is undergraduate students who want a clearer view of their course standing, assignment performance, and overall academic progress during the term.

What can I do on the site?

You can review your dashboard, add courses, enter assignment grades and weights, use the What-If Calculator on a course page, estimate your GPA, and create a university profile that lets you sign in on this device.

Topic 02

Grade Tracking Questions

How is my current course grade calculated?

GradeGuard uses a weighted course calculation. Each assignment score is multiplied by the percentage weight it contributes to the course, and the weighted results are added together.

What does completed course weight mean?

Completed course weight is the total percentage of the course that has already been graded. For example, if three assignments worth 15% each have been marked, then 45% of the course weight has been completed.

Why do I see green, yellow, or red status labels?

These labels provide quick visual feedback about current performance. They are paired with text so colour is not the only indicator.

How does the What-If Calculator help me?

The What-If Calculator compares your current weighted standing with your target final grade. It then estimates the score you need on remaining assessments to reach that goal.

Topic 03

GPA Questions

How does GradeGuard convert percentages into GPA points?

The GPA Overview page shows the 4.0 scale and conversion chart. Each course percentage is matched to a letter grade and GPA value, then used in the overall GPA calculation.

Why do credit hours matter in the GPA calculation?

GPA is weighted by credit hours. Courses worth more credits have a larger impact on your final GPA than courses worth fewer credits.

What formula is used for overall GPA?

GradeGuard multiplies each course GPA value by its credit hours, adds those weighted points, and then divides by the total number of credit hours.

Topic 04

Profile and Privacy Questions

What information belongs on the profile page?

The profile page is designed for university-specific information such as your student name, student number, program, year of study, preferred GPA scale, contact email, and academic target settings.

Do I need to use my university email?

GradeGuard encourages university email addresses because it is designed for student use, but any valid email address can be used to create your on-device account.

Will my information be permanently saved right now?

Yes. Your courses, assignments, and profile are saved in this browser using localStorage, and your password is stored only as a one-way hash. Nothing is sent to a server, and clearing your browser data removes it all.

Topic 05

Troubleshooting Questions

Why did my saved courses or profile disappear?

GradeGuard stores everything in this browser's localStorage. Clearing browsing data, using private browsing, or switching to another browser or device starts you fresh. Use the Restore sample data button on My Courses to bring back the example courses.

What if my assignment weights add up to more than 100%?

GradeGuard will not let a new assignment push a course past 100%. The form shows an error that tells you exactly how much weight remains ungraded, so your course breakdown always matches the official syllabus.

What should I do if I cannot find a feature?

Start from the dashboard, then use the navigation menu to move to My Courses, Course Details, GPA Overview, GPA Calculator, Profile, or About / Help. The FAQ page also links to the main student tools below.
