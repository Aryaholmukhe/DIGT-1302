Tips for Managing Your Courses

  • Add all your courses to get the most accurate GPA summary on the GPA Overview and GPA Calculator pages.
  • Set a target grade for each course so you can track whether you are on track throughout the term.
  • Click "View Details" on any course to jump to its matching course details section and review assignments.
  • Update regularly: Add new assignment scores as soon as you receive them to keep your course progress current.
  • Use the What-If Calculator on the Course Details page to plan for final exams and major projects.
