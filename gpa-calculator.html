Sample GPA Breakdown

The table below shows an example of how courses are weighted by credit hours when calculating an overall GPA.

Example Weighted GPA Calculation
Course Percentage GPA Points Credit Hours Weighted Points
DIGT 1302 86% 3.8 3 11.4
Introduction to Psychology 74% 3.0 3 9.0
Statistics 78% 3.3 4 13.2
Totals 10 33.6

Overall GPA: 33.6 weighted points divided by 10 credit hours = 3.36 GPA

GPA Formula

To calculate weighted GPA, GradeGuard uses this formula:

Overall GPA = Total Weighted GPA Points ÷ Total Credit Hours

Weighted GPA points are found by multiplying each course's GPA points by its credit hours.
