Purpose of the Website

GradeGuard is a client-side web application designed to help university students understand and manage their academic performance. Instead of using confusing spreadsheets or doing manual calculations, students can enter course grades, assignment weights, and credit hours to see a clearer picture of their progress.

The goal of GradeGuard is to reduce stress by giving students quick feedback about their current standing, future grade goals, and overall GPA.

Main Features

Dashboard

The dashboard shows an overview of all courses, current grades, GPA, and academic status. Courses are shown with clear status labels such as green, yellow, or red depending on the current grade.

Course Management

Students can create course profiles and add assignment information, including assignment name, score, and weight toward the final course grade.

What-If Calculator

The What-If Calculator helps students plan ahead by calculating what score is needed on remaining assessments to reach a target final grade.

GPA Overview and Calculator

GradeGuard separates GPA help into two pages. The GPA Overview explains the 4.0 scale, conversion chart, and weighted formula, while the GPA Calculator lets students enter their own course percentages and credit hours.

How to Use GradeGuard

  1. Start on the dashboard and review your current course summary.
  2. Add a new course by entering the course name and credit hours.
  3. Open the course details page to add assignments, scores, and weights.
  4. Use the What-If Calculator to test different final grade goals.
  5. Visit the GPA Overview page first, then use the GPA Calculator page to estimate your overall GPA.

Grading Logic

Weighted Course Grade

A course grade is calculated by multiplying each assignment score by its weight and then adding the weighted values together. For example, if an assignment is worth 20% of the course and the student earns 80%, that assignment contributes 16 percentage points to the final grade.

Completed Course Weight

Completed course weight is the total percentage of the course that has already been graded. This helps students understand how much of the course mark is already known.

What-If Calculation

The What-If Calculator compares the student's current weighted grade with the desired final grade. It then calculates the score needed on the remaining course weight to reach the target.

GPA Calculation

The GPA Calculator converts each course percentage into a GPA value and then weights that GPA value by the course credit hours. The final GPA is the total weighted GPA points divided by the total credit hours.

Accessibility and Design Goals

  • Pages use semantic HTML elements such as header, nav, main, section, article, and footer.
  • Forms use labels so users can clearly understand each input field.
  • Colour is not the only indicator of status; text labels are also provided.
  • The planned design will be responsive for both desktop and mobile screens.
