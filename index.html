Getting started

How to Use GradeGuard

View quick setup steps
  1. Add a course by entering the course name and credit hours.
  2. Open the course detail page and add each assignment, score, and weight.
  3. Check your current weighted grade and completed course weight.
  4. Use the What-If Calculator to choose a target final grade.
  5. Review the GPA Overview, then use the GPA Calculator to see how your courses affect your overall GPA.

What to expect

Feedback You Will See

View feedback and alerts
  • Form validation: inline warnings for missing, negative, or invalid grade values.
  • Performance indicators: green, yellow, and red grade status labels.
  • Progress bars: visual summaries of completed course weight.
  • Dynamic grade messages: the What-If Calculator explains what score is needed to reach a target grade.
  • Confirmation messages: instant updates after courses or assignments are added or removed.
