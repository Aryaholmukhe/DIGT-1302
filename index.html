Welcome to Your Academic Dashboard

GradeGuard is a student-focused grade tracking tool that helps you calculate your current course standing, plan future assessment scores, and understand your overall GPA.

Add your courses, enter assignment grades and weights, then use GradeGuard to see where you stand and what you need to reach your target final grade.

Quick Tools

  • Manage a Course - add assignments, enter scores, and view course progress.
  • What-If Calculator - find out what score you need on remaining assessments.
  • GPA Calculator - convert course percentages into GPA points and calculate your weighted GPA.
  • University Profile - manage your profile and sign-in information.
  • FAQ - read common questions about grade tracking, GPA, and account setup.

How to Use GradeGuard

  1. Add a course by entering the course name and credit hours.
  2. Open the course detail page and add each assignment, score, and weight.
  3. Check your current weighted grade and completed course weight.
  4. Use the What-If Calculator to choose a target final grade.
  5. Review the GPA Calculator to see how your courses affect your overall GPA.

Feedback You Will See

  • Form validation: warnings for missing, negative, or invalid grade values.
  • Performance indicators: green, yellow, and red grade status labels.
  • Progress bars: visual summaries of completed course weight.
  • Dynamic grade messages: clear explanations of what score is needed to reach a target grade.
  • Confirmation messages: updates after courses or assignments are added or removed.
